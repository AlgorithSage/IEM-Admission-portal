Frequently Asked Questions

What marks do I need?

It depends on the stream. See the Courses, Eligibility & Fees table above. The form checks your marks against your chosen streams.

What happens if a document is rejected?

You get an email with the reason. Replace the document from your application page and resubmit.

Can I choose more than one stream?

Yes. You apply for one program and can rank up to three streams for B.Tech and two for M.Tech or Management.
