APPLICANT CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum academic percentage required?

Under AICTE and institutional norms, applicants must have secured at least 45.0% aggregate marks in their qualifying examination (10+2 or equivalent).

How soon will my application be reviewed?

The IEM Admission Scrutiny Cell reviews applications on a rolling basis. Application status updates are reflected in your dashboard within 48 to 72 business hours.

Can I apply for multiple programs?

Applicants may select their primary desired program during initial submission. Cross-program counseling options are available during the verification phase.
